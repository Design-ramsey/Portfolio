Biography

I am a multidisciplinary Graphic Designer specializing in brand identity, UI/UX design, and technical print production. Operating at the intersection of digital environments and physical media, my process ensures that every concept translates seamlessly from screen to real-world application.

From crafting intuitive web interfaces in Figma to configuring precise packaging dielines and executing large-format print runs, I manage design lifecycles end-to-end. Currently, I balance my BTech in Graphic Design at Takoradi Technical University with an active freelance practice, continuously exploring new visual narratives.
